Commercial & statutory terms

The 70/30 split

Everything on this screen is the 30% — configured per client at deployment. Everything behind it is the 70%: Connect onboarding, direct-charge fund flow, Checkout, server-driven Terminal, pre-auth tabs, refund clawback and reconciliation, all shipped unchanged.

Also per-client but set at onboarding time: dashboard mode (Stripe-hosted vs API-based), payout cadence, and enabled payment methods per market.

Not everything flows through Stripe. Delivery aggregators and OTAs collect payment themselves and remit net proceeds separately — those orders carry no PaymentIntent and no platform fee, and need their own reconciliation path against the aggregator's settlement file. Assuming all revenue moves through Stripe is the most common scoping error in an F&B platform engagement.
